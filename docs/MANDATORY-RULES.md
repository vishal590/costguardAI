## 🚨 Critical Mandatory Requirements

### Before You Start - MUST DO
- ✅ **Read Terms and Conditions:** [competition/terms.md](./competition/terms.md)
- ✅ **Verify Eligibility:** Must be 18+, have Builder ID, reside in eligible country
- ✅ **Check Excluded Countries:** See [competition/eligibility.md](./competition/eligibility.md)
- ✅ **One Entry Per Person:** Limit one idea per person
- ✅ **Submit Before Deadline:** Must submit by January 21, 2026 11:59pm PT
- ✅ **Submit Early (Recommended):** AWS evaluates "first 10,000+ submissions received" - submitting early ensures evaluation
- ⚠️ **No Public Counter:** There's no way to check how many submissions have been received - submit early to be safe

### Initial Submission - MUST INCLUDE
- ⚠️ **NO CODE REQUIRED** - Written pitch only (idea pitch, not MVP)
- ✅ **Project Description:** What you want to build
- ✅ **Detailed Kiro Implementation Documentation** ⚠️ **REQUIRED**
- ✅ **AWS Services List:** Services you plan to use
- ✅ **Market Impact:** Potential impact description

### If Selected as First-Round Finalist - MUST DO
- ✅ **Use Kiro:** Must use Kiro for at least portion of development
- ✅ **Stay in Free Tier:** Must not exceed AWS Free Tier limits
- ✅ **Original Application:** Must be original and unpublished
- ✅ **Publish Article:** Must publish Builder Center article by March 13, 2026 11:59pm PT (4 weeks from announcement)
- ✅ **Article Content:** Article MUST describe development process, technical architecture, and key learnings
- ✅ **Document Everything:** Must document AWS services and Kiro usage

### If Selected as Winner - MUST DO
- ✅ **Respond to Communications:** Must respond within reasonable time (as determined by AWS)
- ✅ **Provide Tax Documentation:** W-9 (US) or W-8 (non-US) - REQUIRED before receiving cash prize
- ✅ **Register for AWS Credits:** Must register through AWS Credit Portal to receive promotional credits
- ✅ **Accept Promotional Use:** Permission for AWS to use name/likeness (constitutes permission by accepting prize)
- ✅ **Confirm Prize Acceptance:** Confirm receipt is not prohibited, creates no conflict of interest, etc.

## ❌ PROHIBITED - MUST NOT DO

### Eligibility Prohibitions
- ❌ **Excluded Countries:** Cannot be from Argentina, Brazil, Italy, Philippines, Russia, Thailand, Vietnam, Cuba, Iran, Syria, North Korea, Crimea
- ❌ **AWS Employees:** Cannot be AWS employee or family member
- ❌ **Multiple Submissions:** Cannot submit more than one idea per person

### Submission Prohibitions
- ❌ **Exceed Free Tier:** Cannot exceed AWS Free Tier limits
- ❌ **Published Apps:** Cannot submit previously published application
- ❌ **Illegal Activities:** Cannot perform/promote illegal activities
- ❌ **IP Violations:** Cannot violate intellectual property, proprietary, or other rights of others
- ❌ **Offensive Content:** Cannot perform/promote activities that are offensive or disparaging
- ❌ **Harmful Content:** Cannot cause harm to others or to AWS's operations or reputation

---

## ⚠️ Consequences of Non-Compliance

- **Disqualification:** Entries not meeting requirements may be disqualified
- **Forfeiture:** Failure to respond may result in prize forfeiture
- **Liability:** Participants responsible for compliance with all terms

## Five-Stage Process

The competition follows **five distinct stages**:

### Stage 1: Initial Submission

**Deadline:** January 21, 2026 11:59pm PT

**What You Submit:**
- Written pitch describing:
  - What you want to build (Project Description)
  - How you plan to build it
  - What the market impact or potential could be
  - AWS Free Tier services you'll use
  - **Detailed documentation of Kiro implementation** ⚠️ **REQUIRED**

**Requirements:**
- No code required
- Focus on selling your vision
- Keep it focused and compelling
- Must include detailed Kiro implementation documentation

**What Happens Next:**
- AWS evaluates first 10,000+ submissions received
- Evaluation period: January 21, 2026 9:00am PT to February 11, 2026 5:00pm PT
- Top 1,000 First-Round Finalists identified

---

### Stage 2: First-Round Finalists Selected

**Announcement Date:** February 11, 2026 5:00pm PT

**Selection Process:**
- AWS evaluates first 10,000+ Initial Submissions received
- Evaluation using combination of AI tools and human review
- Judging criteria: Project Completeness (50%), Content Creativity (50%)
- Top 1,000 First-Round Finalists identified

**If Selected:**
- You have **4 weeks** to publish Builder Center article (from Feb 11 to Mar 13, 2026)
- Article deadline: **March 13, 2026 11:59pm PT**
- ⚠️ **Article MUST describe:** Development process, technical architecture, and key learnings (per Terms and Conditions)
- You must build your application
- Must use Kiro for at least part of development
- Must stay within AWS Free Tier limits
- Must be original and unpublished
- Must publish Builder Center article

---

### Stage 3: Semi-Finalist Voting

**Article Submission Deadline:** March 13, 2026 11:59pm PT  
**Voting Period:** March 13, 2026 12:01am PT to March 19, 2026 11:59pm PT  
**Advancement:** March 20, 2026 9:00am PT (Top 300 Semi-Finalists announced)

**What You Do:**
- Publish Builder Center article explaining your application
- Article must include:
  - Development process
  - Technical architecture
  - Key learnings
  - Application overview and why it matters
  - AWS services used
  - Team name
  - Demo

**What Happens:**
- Community votes on articles (based on number of likes)
- Top 300 Semi-Finalists advance to next stage

---

### Stage 4: AWS Experts Review

**Review Period:** March 20, 2026 9:00am PT to April 3, 2026 5:00pm PT  
**Announcement Date:** April 3, 2026 5:00pm PT

**Selection Process:**
- Panel of AWS Experts and AWS Heroes review submissions
- Judging criteria:
  - Technical Innovation (34%)
  - Implementation Quality (33%)
  - Market Impact (33%)
- 50 Finalists selected

**If Selected as Finalist:**
- You advance to final voting stage
- Opportunity to revise article: April 3, 2026 9:00am PT to April 30, 2026 5:00pm PT

---

### Stage 5: Finalists Voting

**Article Revision Period:** April 3, 2026 9:00am PT to April 30, 2026 5:00pm PT  
**Note:** Official Terms state "April 3, 2025" but this appears to be a typo; revision period is April 3, 2026 to April 30, 2026  
**Final Voting:** April 3-30, 2026  
**Winners Announced:** April 30, 2026 5:00pm PT via article posted on AWS Builder Center

**What Happens:**
- Finalists can revise articles
- Final community voting (based on number of likes)
- Voters analyze:
  - Technical Innovation
  - Market Impact
  - Implementation Quality
  - Global Scalability
- Winners determined and announced via article on AWS Builder Center

**Winners Receive:**
- Cash prizes (credited by May 30, 2026)
- AWS credits (credited by May 30, 2026)
- Recognition at AWS re:Invent 2026
- Showcase at AWS Summits worldwide (April-September 2026)

## Initial Submission Requirements ⚠️ MANDATORY

**Deadline:** January 21, 2026 11:59pm PT  
**Format:** Written pitch (no code required at this stage)

**⚠️ IMPORTANT:** By submitting, you agree to be bound by the [Terms and Conditions](../competition/terms.md). Non-compliance may result in disqualification.

### Required Information

Your submission form **must include** (per Terms and Conditions):

1. **Project Description**
   - What you want to build
   - Why it matters
   - How your app will make a real difference
   - Keep it focused and compelling

2. **Detailed Documentation of Kiro Implementation** ⚠️ **REQUIRED**
   - How you plan to use Kiro
   - Portion of development using Kiro
   - Kiro features you'll leverage
   - Integration approach

3. **AWS Services to be Used**
   - List of AWS Free Tier services you plan to use
   - Brief rationale for why you are using those services

4. **Market Impact**
   - What the market impact or potential could be
   - How you plan to build it

### Submission Focus

**Remember:** This is about selling your vision, not building it yet. Focus on:
- Clear description of your idea
- Compelling value proposition
- Feasibility within AWS Free Tier
- Real-world impact potential

## Semi-Finalist Requirements

If selected as a semi-finalist (top 1,000), you must:

### Application Requirements

1. **Use Kiro**
   - Must use Kiro for at least a portion of development

2. **AWS Free Tier Only**
   - Use the AWS Free Tier only
   - Do not exceed free tier limits
   - Applications judged on innovation, efficiency, and practical impact—not computational scale

3. **Original Application**
   - Be an original, new, and unpublished application

4. **Builder Center Article**
   - Publish an article on Builder Center (see [article-requirements.md](./article-requirements.md))

## Builder Center Article Requirements

**When:** Required if selected as semi-finalist  
**Deadline:** March 13, 2026 (for initial article)

### Required Content

Your Builder Center article must include:

1. **Application Overview**
   - Overview of your application
   - Why it matters

2. **AWS Services Documentation**
   - List of AWS services you used
   - Brief justification for each service
   - Resource optimization strategies
   - Expected running costs

3. **Team Information**
   - Your team's name

4. **Demo**
   - Demo of your app

### Article Updates

- **Initial Article:** Due March 13, 2026 (semi-finalist phase)
- **Revised Article:** Due April 17, 2026 (finalist phase, if selected)

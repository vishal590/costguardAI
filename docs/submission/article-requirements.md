## Article Overview

**When Required:** If selected as First-Round Finalist (top 1,000)  
**Initial Deadline:** March 13, 2026 11:59pm PT  
**Revised Deadline:** April 30, 2026 5:00pm PT (if selected as finalist)

**Time Allotted:** 4 weeks from First-Round Finalist announcement (February 11, 2026) to article deadline

**Purpose:** Showcase your application, AWS services used, and demonstrate your solution to the community and judges.

**⚠️ MANDATORY:** Article must describe your **development process, technical architecture, and key learnings** (per Terms and Conditions).

---

## Required Content ⚠️ MANDATORY

**Per Terms and Conditions, your article MUST describe:**
- **Development process**
- **Technical architecture**
- **Key learnings**

Your Builder Center article must include the following sections:

### 1. Application Overview

**Required:**
- Overview of your application
- Why it matters
- Problem it solves
- Value proposition

**Tips:**
- Make it compelling and clear
- Explain the real-world impact
- Connect to your chosen competition track

---

### 2. AWS Services Documentation

**Required Information:**
- List of AWS services you used
- Brief justification for each service
- Resource optimization strategies
- Expected running costs

**Details for Each Service:**
- Service name
- Why you chose this service
- How it's used in your application
- Free Tier usage details

**Example Structure:**
```
### AWS Services Used

1. **Amazon Lambda**
   - **Purpose:** Serverless compute for API endpoints
   - **Justification:** Cost-effective, auto-scaling, fits Free Tier
   - **Usage:** Handles user requests and API calls
   - **Free Tier:** 1M requests/month
   - **Expected Cost:** $0 (within Free Tier)

2. **Amazon DynamoDB**
   - **Purpose:** NoSQL database for user data
   - **Justification:** Fast, scalable, serverless
   - **Usage:** Stores user profiles and application data
   - **Free Tier:** 25 GB storage, 25 read/write units
   - **Expected Cost:** $0 (within Free Tier)
```

---

### 3. Team Information

**Required:**
- Your team's name
- Team members (if applicable)

**Optional:**
- Brief team bio
- Team background

---

### 4. Demo

**Required:**
- Demo of your app
- Can be video, screenshots, or interactive demo

**Demo Should Show:**
- Key features of your application
- How it works
- User experience
- Value it provides

**Demo Formats:**
- Video (recommended): YouTube, Vimeo, or embedded
- Screenshots: With captions explaining features
- Interactive: Live demo link (if accessible)
- GIFs: Animated demonstrations

## Article Structure Template

```markdown
# [Your Application Name]

## Overview
[Application overview and why it matters]

## Problem Statement
[What problem does it solve?]

## Solution
[How does your application solve it?]

## AWS Services Used

### Service 1: [Name]
- **Purpose:** [What it's used for]
- **Justification:** [Why you chose it]
- **Usage:** [How it's used]
- **Free Tier:** [Free Tier details]
- **Expected Cost:** [Cost estimate]

[Repeat for each service]

## Resource Optimization
[Strategies you used to stay within Free Tier]

## Expected Running Costs
[Breakdown of costs, showing $0 if within Free Tier]

## Demo
[Demo video, screenshots, or interactive demo]

## Team
[Team name and members]

## Conclusion
[Summary and future plans]
```

## Article Quality Guidelines

### Writing
- Clear and concise
- Well-structured with headings
- Professional tone
- Free of spelling/grammar errors

### Technical Accuracy
- Accurate AWS service information
- Correct Free Tier limits
- Realistic cost estimates
- Working demo

### Visuals
- High-quality screenshots
- Clear demo video
- Professional presentation
- Helpful diagrams (if applicable)

## Article Updates

### Initial Article (First-Round Finalist)
- **Due:** March 13, 2026 11:59pm PT
- **Time Allotted:** 4 weeks from announcement (Feb 11 - Mar 13, 2026)
- **Purpose:** Community voting
- **Focus:** Complete and compelling
- **Must Include:** Development process, technical architecture, key learnings

### Revised Article (Finalist)
- **Revision Period:** April 3, 2026 9:00am PT to April 30, 2026 5:00pm PT
- **Note:** Per Terms and Conditions, revision period starts April 3, 2026 (not April 17)
- **Purpose:** Final judging
- **Focus:** Incorporate improvements, polish

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ Exceeding Free Tier limits
- ❌ Not using Kiro
- ❌ Incomplete AWS services documentation
- ❌ Missing demo
- ❌ Unclear value proposition
- ❌ Poor article structure
- ❌ Inaccurate cost estimates

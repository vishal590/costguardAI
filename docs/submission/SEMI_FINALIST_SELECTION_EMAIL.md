## Guidelines for Creating Your Article

When writing your article, include:

### 1) Required sections

- **App category** – List the category your project aligns with: Workplace Efficiency, Daily Life Enhancement, Commercial Solutions, Social Impact, or Creative Expression.
- **My vision** – Explain what your idea is and what you built.
- **Why this matters** – Explain why your idea matters to people. Tie it back to the app category you selected.
- **How I built this** – Share your technical approach, architecture, and key development milestones.
- **Demo** – Share screenshots of your application, or create a video that is **less than 5 minutes** and embed it (e.g. upload to YouTube and use the **Insert YouTube embed** feature).
- **What I learned** – Explain what you learned. Include key insights gained as they relate to your development journey.

### 2) Cover image

- Add a **cover image** – something that captures attention.

### 3) Article title

- Use: **"AIdeas: [your project or app name]"**  
- For CostGuard AI: **AIdeas: CostGuard AI**

### 4) Tags

Select:
1. **#aideas-2025**
2. Your **app category tag**: #workplace-efficiency, #daily-life-enhancement, **#commercial-solutions**, #social-impact, or #creative-expression
3. Your **region tag**: #EMEA, #NAMER, #APJC, #LATAM, #GCR, or #ANZ

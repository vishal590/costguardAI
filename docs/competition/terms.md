## Evaluation Process

### First-Round Finalists Selection
- **Evaluation Period:** January 21, 2026 9:00am PT to February 11, 2026 5:00pm PT
- **Selection:** AWS will evaluate the **first 10,000+ Initial Submissions that were received** during Initial Submission Period
- **Note:** The "+" suggests AWS may evaluate more than 10,000 if received, but this is not guaranteed. If more than 10,000 submissions are received, only the first 10,000+ may be evaluated.
- **Result:** 1,000 First-Round Finalists selected
- **Article Deadline:** March 13, 2026 11:59pm PT
- **Time Allotted:** 4 weeks from announcement (February 11, 2026) to article deadline
- **Article Must Describe:** Development process, technical architecture, and key learnings

### Community Voting (Semi-Finalists)
- **Voting Period:** March 13, 2026 12:01am PT to March 19, 2026 11:59pm PT
- **Result:** Top 300 Semi-Finalists announced March 20, 2026 9:00am PT

### Finalist Selection
- **Selection Period:** March 20, 2026 9:00am PT to April 3, 2026 5:00pm PT
- **Result:** 50 Finalists announced April 3, 2026 5:00pm PT

### Finalist Article Revision
- **Revision Period:** April 3, 2026 9:00am PT to April 30, 2026 5:00pm PT
- **Note:** Finalists have opportunity to revise their article submission
- **⚠️ Note:** Official Terms state "April 3, 2025" but this appears to be a typo; revision period is April 3, 2026 to April 30, 2026

### Winners Announcement
- **Date:** April 30, 2026 5:00pm PT
- **Method:** Via article posted on AWS Builder Center

## Technical Criteria

Participants must:
1. **Use Kiro** for a portion of their application development
2. **Build within AWS Free Tier limits**
3. **Create an original application** that has not been previously published
4. **Document their use of AWS services and Kiro** in their submission

## Judging Criteria

### First-Round Finalists
Evaluated by AWS using a combination of AI tools and human review analyzing:
- **Project Completeness (50%)**
- **Content Creativity (50%)**

### Semi-Finalists
Evaluated by AWS using community voting process held on AWS Builder Center based on:
- **Articles with the highest number of likes**

### Finalists
Evaluated by a panel of AWS experts and AWS Heroes analyzing:
- **Technical Innovation (34%)**
- **Implementation Quality (33%)**
- **Market Impact (33%)**

### Winners
Evaluated by AWS using community voting process held on AWS Builder Center based on:
- **Articles with the highest number of likes**

Voters will determine Challenge winners by analyzing:
- Technical Innovation
- Market Impact
- Implementation Quality
- Global Scalability

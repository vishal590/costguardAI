## Critical Submission Details

### First 10,000+ Submissions

**Official Terms State:** "AWS will evaluate the first 10,000+ Initial Submissions that were received"

**Interpretation:** The wording is somewhat ambiguous, but it appears AWS will evaluate at least the first 10,000 submissions received chronologically during the Initial Submission Period.

**What This Means:**
- If fewer than 10,000 submissions are received: All submissions will be evaluated
- If more than 10,000 submissions are received: AWS will evaluate at least the first 10,000+ submissions (the "+" suggests they may evaluate more, but this is not guaranteed)

**Recommendation:** 
- **Submit before the deadline** (January 21, 2026 11:59pm PT) - this is mandatory
- **Submit early if possible** - to ensure your submission is among those evaluated (especially if many submissions are expected)
- **Do not wait until the last minute** - technical issues or delays could cause your submission to arrive after the first 10,000+

**Note:** The exact interpretation of "first 10,000+" is not entirely clear from the Terms. It's safest to submit as early as possible within the submission period.

**⚠️ Important:** There is **no public dashboard or counter** where you can check how many submissions have been received. AWS does not provide real-time submission numbers to participants. You will only know if you were among the first 10,000+ when First-Round Finalists are announced on February 11, 2026.

## Tax Obligations

### Before Receiving Cash Prize

**Required:** Winners must provide all requested tax documentation **before receiving any cash prize**:
- **U.S. persons:** IRS Form W-9
- **Non-U.S. persons:** Appropriate IRS Form W-8 series

**Tax Reporting:**
- Cash prizes may be subject to tax reporting (IRS Forms 1099 or 1042-S)
- All prize amounts are stated as **gross amounts** before tax withholding
- Winners receive **net amount** after withholding (if any)

**Recommendation:** Consult your own tax advisor regarding tax consequences.

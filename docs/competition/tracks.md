## Five Competition Tracks

The competition offers **five tracks** targeting key opportunity areas. You must select one track for your application.

### 1. Workplace Efficiency

**Focus Areas:**
- Automating routine tasks
- Enhancing collaboration
- Improving productivity workflows

**Examples:**
- Task automation tools
- Team collaboration platforms
- Workflow optimization apps
- Meeting assistants
- Document management systems

### 2. Daily Life Enhancement

**Focus Areas:**
- Smart home applications
- Personal assistant tools
- Lifestyle optimization

**Examples:**
- Home automation systems
- Personal productivity assistants
- Health and wellness apps
- Meal planning tools
- Schedule optimization apps

### 3. Commercial Potential

**Focus Areas:**
- Industry-specific solutions
- Enterprise applications
- Revenue-generating innovations

**Examples:**
- B2B SaaS solutions
- Industry automation tools
- Enterprise productivity apps
- Business intelligence tools
- Customer service automation

### 4. Social Good

**Focus Areas:**
- Environmental impact
- Healthcare accessibility
- Education technology

**Examples:**
- Climate change solutions
- Healthcare access tools
- Educational platforms
- Accessibility applications
- Community support systems

### 5. Personal Expression (Creative Track)

**Focus Areas:**
- Creative tools
- Digital art applications
- Content creation

**Examples:**
- AI art generators
- Content creation tools
- Creative writing assistants
- Music composition tools
- Video editing assistants

## Track Selection

**Important:** Choose the track that best aligns with your application idea. Your selection should be clear in your submission.

**Note:** There is a **Creative Track - Special Achievement Award** specifically for exceptional creativity, unique use cases, or inspiring social impact (see [prizes.md](./prizes.md)).

## Competition Overview

The Global 10,000 AIdeas Competition is **one of the largest developer competitions in AWS history**, inviting developers worldwide to build innovative AI applications using AWS tools.

**What Participants Gain:**
- Hands-on experience with next-gen AI development tools
- Connect with innovators globally
- Access technical enablement through:
  - Bi-weekly workshops
  - AWS User Groups
  - AWS Builder Center resources

**Competition Rewards:**
- **$250,000 in cash prizes**
- **AWS credits**
- **Recognition from AWS** across 20 winner categories
- Recognition may include:
  - Featured placement at AWS re:Invent 2026
  - Featured placement across AWS channels
  - Opportunities to connect with technical leaders

**Participation Format:**
- **Worldwide participation** - Open to developers worldwide (subject to eligibility)
- Submissions made **online** through AWS Builder Center
- Development uses **AWS Free Tier** (cloud-based) and **Kiro**
- Judging and voting done **online** through Builder Center
- Articles published **online** on Builder Center
- **Note:** Format not explicitly stated as "virtual" in official sources, but all processes are conducted online/remotely

## How This Works

You are tasked with **pitching an application idea** for one of the five competition tracks:
1. Workplace Efficiency
2. Daily Life Enhancement
3. Commercial Solutions
4. Social Impact
5. Creative Expression

### Initial Submission Phase

**Your initial submission is a written pitch—no code required at this stage—that describes:**
- What you want to build
- Why it matters

**When joining the challenge, your submission form should clearly describe:**
- Your idea
- Which AWS Free Tier services you'll use
- How your app will make a real difference

**Focus:** Keep it focused and compelling. **This is about selling your vision, not building it yet.**

### Semi-Finalist Phase

**If selected as a semi-finalist, that's when you'll build your app.**

Your finished app needs to:
- Use **Kiro** for at least part of development
- Stay within **AWS Free Tier limits**
- Be completely **original and not yet published**

Semi-finalists will also publish a **Builder Center article** showcasing:
- Their app
- The AWS services used
- Their team's name
- A demo

## Get Started

Follow these steps to participate:

1. **Sign into Builder Center and complete your Builder Center profile**
2. **Join the Builder Center official 10,000 AIdeas Competition space** for announcements, community connection and resources
3. **Create your free AWS account**
4. **Download Kiro**
5. **Start ideating!**

**Get your submission in by January 21, 2026**

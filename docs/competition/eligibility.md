## Eligibility Criteria ⚠️ MANDATORY

**By participating, you must comply with all eligibility requirements. Non-compliance will result in disqualification.**

The competition is open to **individuals or teams of up to four** participants.

### Required Criteria

Participants must meet **all** of the following:

1. **Age Requirement:** Be 18 years or older
2. **AWS Builder ID:** Have a valid AWS Builder ID
3. **Kiro Usage:** Use Kiro (required tool)
4. **AWS Free Tier:** Comply with AWS Free Tier terms of service
5. **Residency:** Reside in an eligible country

**Excluded Countries/Regions:**
The following countries and regions are **EXCLUDED** from participation:
- Argentina
- Brazil
- Italy
- Philippines
- Russia
- Thailand
- Vietnam
- Cuba
- Iran
- Syria
- North Korea
- **Crimea region**

### Participation Details

- **Team Size:** Up to 4 members per team
- **Experience Level:** Open to both new AWS users and existing AWS builders
- **AWS Account:** Must have an AWS account

## Restrictions

### Not Eligible

The following are **NOT eligible** to participate:
- AWS employees
- Immediate family members of AWS employees
- Members of AWS employees' households

## Submission Limit

**One idea per person** - This ensures everyone has an equal opportunity to be selected and encourages you to focus on your single best concept!

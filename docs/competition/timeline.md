## Submission Process Stages

The competition follows **five stages**:

1. **Initial Submission** (Due: January 21, 2026 11:59pm PT)
   - Written pitch describing what you want to build
   - How you plan to build it
   - Market impact or potential
   - AWS Free Tier services you'll use
   - **Detailed documentation of Kiro implementation**

2. **First-Round Finalists Selected** (Announced: February 11, 2026 5:00pm PT)
   - AWS evaluates first 10,000+ submissions (Jan 21 - Feb 11, 2026)
   - Top 1,000 First-Round Finalists identified
   - 4 weeks to publish article (by March 13, 2026 11:59pm PT)

3. **Semi-Finalist Voting** (March 13-19, 2026)
   - First-Round Finalists publish Builder Center article
   - Community votes on articles (based on likes)
   - Top 300 Semi-Finalists announced (March 20, 2026 9:00am PT)

4. **Finalist Selection** (March 20 - April 3, 2026)
   - AWS Experts and AWS Heroes review submissions
   - 50 Finalists announced (April 3, 2026 5:00pm PT)

5. **Finalists Voting** (April 3-30, 2026)
   - Finalists can revise articles (April 3, 2026 9:00am PT - April 30, 2026 5:00pm PT)
   - Final community voting
   - Winners determined and announced (April 30, 2026 5:00pm PT)

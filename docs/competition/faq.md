## General Questions

### Is this a virtual or in-person hackathon?

**Answer:** The official Terms and website **do not explicitly state** "virtual" or "in-person," but the competition structure indicates it can be participated in **remotely/online**.

**What We Know:**
- **Worldwide participation** - Open to developers worldwide (subject to eligibility)
- **Submissions** are made **online** through AWS Builder Center
- **Development** uses **AWS Free Tier** (cloud-based) and **Kiro** (can be used remotely)
- **Judging and voting** are done **online** through Builder Center
- **Articles** are published **online** on Builder Center
- **Support resources:**
  - Bi-weekly technical workshops across all major time zones (likely online)
  - AWS User Group meetups for local collaboration (may be in-person or online)
  - Weekly blogs and tutorials on AWS Builder Center (online)
  - Community forums for peer support (online)
- **No physical locations or venues** mentioned for participation
- Timeline and submission process designed to accommodate remote participation

**Winners Showcase (Optional):**
- Winners may be invited to showcase at AWS Summits worldwide (May-September 2026)
- Winners may be featured at AWS re:Invent 2026
- **Note:** Attending these events is optional and not required for participation or winning

**Conclusion:** While not explicitly stated as "virtual," the competition structure suggests it can be participated in **entirely online/remotely**. There is no mention of required physical attendance for participation.

### Who can participate in the challenge?

**Answer:** Any individual or team of up to four can participate, whether they are new to AWS, or existing AWS builders. Participants must have an AWS account and meet the following criteria:

1. **Be 18 years or older**
2. **Have a valid AWS Builder ID**
3. **Use Kiro** (required tool)
4. **Comply with AWS Free Tier terms of service**
5. **Reside in an eligible country**

**Not Eligible:**
- AWS employees
- Immediate family members of AWS employees
- Members of AWS employees' households

**Team Size:** Up to 4 members per team

## Submission Process Questions

### How do submissions progress through the competition?

**Answer:** The competition follows **five stages**:

1. **Initial submission** (Due: January 21, 2026)
   - Participants must tell what they want to build
   - How they plan to build it
   - What the market impact or potential could be
   - Submissions are due by January 21, 2026

2. **Semi-finalists selected** (Announced: February 11, 2026)
   - AWS will review, rank, and score all submissions
   - Top 1,000 semi-finalists (First-Round Finalists) identified

3. **Semi-finalist voting** (March 13-19, 2026)
   - Semi-finalists share an article on AWS Builder Center to explain their application
   - Community will vote on articles to determine 300 semi-finalists

4. **AWS Experts review** (March 20 - April 3, 2026)
   - AWS Experts will review to select 50 finalists

5. **Finalists voting** (April 3-30, 2026)
   - Final community voting and expert evaluation for winners

## Prize Questions

### What prizes and winning categories are available?

**Answer:** The challenge features a **$250,000 cash prize pool plus $30,000 in AWS Credits** and amazing AWS Builder swag for **20 winning submissions** across the following categories:

#### Professional Track Award - Global Champion (2 winning submissions)
- **Selection:** Determined by community voting from the top 50 finalists
- **Prize:** $25,000 cash each + $5,000 AWS credits each

#### Professional Track Award - Regional Champion (6 winning submissions)
- **Selection:** Highest community-voted finalist from each AWS region (EMEA, NAMER, APJC, LATAM, GCR, ANZ)
- **Prize:** $15,000 cash each ($90,000 total) + $1,500 AWS credits each ($9,000 total)

#### Innovation Award (10 winning submissions)
- **Selection:** Selected by panel of AWS Expert panel for breakthrough technical implementation and creative problem-solving
- **Prize:** $10,000 cash each ($100,000 total) + $1,000 AWS credits each ($10,000 total)

#### Creative Track - Special Achievement Award (2 winning submissions)
- **Selection:** Selected by AWS expert panel for exceptional creativity, unique use cases, or inspiring social impact
- **Prize:** $5,000 cash each ($10,000 total) + $500 AWS credits each ($1,000 total)

## Technical Requirements Questions

### What are the technical requirements of the AIdeas competition?

**Answer:** Your finished application must:

1. **Use Kiro for at least a portion of development** ⚠️ **MANDATORY**
2. **Use the AWS Free Tier only, without exceeding the free tier limits** ⚠️ **MANDATORY**
3. **Be an original, new, and unpublished application** ⚠️ **MANDATORY**
4. **If selected as a semi-finalist, you must publish an article on Builder Center** that includes:
   - Overview of your application and why it matters
   - What AWS services you used
   - Your team name
   - A demo of your app

**Initial Submission Requirements:**
- Project description
- **Detailed documentation of Kiro implementation** ⚠️ **REQUIRED**
- AWS services to be used
- Market impact

## AWS Resources Questions

### What AWS resources can I use for my application?

**Answer:** 

Your task is to **build your application with the AWS Free Tier**. You must also **use Kiro for a portion of your application development**.

**Encouraged AWS Services:**
When you build your app using Kiro agentic IDE on the AWS Free Tier, we encourage you to explore other AWS products and services, including:
- **Amazon Bedrock** - Foundation models and AI capabilities
- **Amazon Bedrock AgentCore** - Agent development
- **Amazon Nova** - Foundational models
- **Strands Agents** - Agent capabilities

**Free Tier Requirement:**
- Applications must stay within AWS Free Tier limits
- Applications are judged on innovation, efficiency, and practical impact—not computational scale

**Judging Focus:**
- Innovation
- Efficiency

## Documentation Questions

### Do I need to document my AWS resource usage?

**Answer:** **Yes. Documentation is required at multiple stages.**

#### Initial Submission (Required)
Your application submission form should include:
- A list of AWS services you **plan to use**
- A brief rationale for why you are using those services

#### Semi-Finalist Article (Required)
If you move to the semi-finals, your AWS Builder Center article must include:
1. **A list of AWS services you used**
2. **A brief justification for each service**
3. **Resource optimization strategies**
4. **Expected running costs**

## Overview

The Global 10,000 AIdeas Competition is one of the largest developer competitions in AWS history, inviting developers worldwide to build innovative AI applications using AWS tools. 

**Benefits:**
- Hands-on experience with next-gen AI development tools
- Connect with innovators globally
- Technical enablement through bi-weekly workshops, AWS User Groups, and AWS Builder Center resources
- Compete for $250,000 in cash prizes, AWS credits, and recognition from AWS across 20 winner categories
- Recognition may include featured placement at AWS re:Invent 2026 and across AWS channels
- Opportunities to connect with technical leaders

---

## How This Works

### Initial Submission (Completed ✅)
You are tasked with pitching an application idea for one of the five competition tracks: workplace efficiency, daily life enhancement, commercial solutions, social impact, and creative expression.

**Initial submission requirements:**
- Written pitch—no code required at this stage
- Describe what you want to build and why it matters
- Clearly describe your idea
- Which AWS Free Tier services you'll use
- How your app will make a real difference
- Keep it focused and compelling
- This is about selling your vision, not building it yet

### Semi-Finalist Phase (Current Stage 🎯)
If selected as a semi-finalist, that's when you'll build your app.

**Requirements:**
- Use Kiro for at least part of development
- Stay within AWS Free Tier limits
- Be completely original and not yet published
- Publish a Builder Center article showcasing:
  - Your app overview
  - AWS services used
  - Team name
  - Demo of your app (format not specified—video, screenshots, or interactive demo are all acceptable; a web frontend is not required)

## Eligibility Requirements

The competition is open to individuals or teams of up to four.

**Participants must:**
1. Be 18 years or older
2. Have a valid AWS Builder ID
3. Use Kiro
4. Comply with AWS Free Tier terms of service
5. Reside in an eligible country

**Exclusions:**
- AWS employees, their immediate family members, and members of their households are not eligible

Read the Terms for full details.

## Competition Stages

The competition follows five stages:

### Stage 1: Initial Submission (Completed ✅)
Participants must tell:
- What they want to build
- How they plan to build it

Submissions were due by January 21, 2026.

### Stage 2: Semi-Finalists Selected (Completed ✅)
AWS reviewed, ranked, and scored all submissions to identify the top 1,000 semi-finalists.

**March 13-19, 2026**

Semi-finalists share an article on AWS Builder Center to explain their application. Community will vote (e.g. by likes) on articles to determine the top 300 semi-finalists. Official guidance: the sooner you publish, the more time you have to earn likes.

**March 20 - April 3, 2026**

AWS Experts will review to select 50 finalists.

**April 17-30, 2026**

Final community voting and expert evaluation for winners.

### Free Tier Limits
- Maximum $200 credits available on Free Tier
- Must build within Free Tier limits

### Recommended AWS Services
When you build your app using Kiro agentic IDE on the AWS Free Tier, you're encouraged to explore:
- Amazon Bedrock
- Amazon Bedrock AgentCore
- Amazon Nova foundational models
- Strands Agents

**Applications are judged on:**
- Innovation

## FAQ Highlights

### Can I submit more than one idea?
No. Please submit one idea per person. This ensures everyone has an equal opportunity to be selected and encourages you to focus on your single best concept.

### What if I have an existing AWS account that is not on the free tier?
No problem! Your idea submission must describe how to build within Free Tier limits (within the maximum $200 credits available on Free Tier). If you advance to the semi-finals and have an existing paid AWS account, AWS will provide you with $200 in credits to build your idea submission so everyone builds under the same conditions.

### Will spending more on AWS services improve my chances of winning?
No. Applications are judged on innovation, efficiency, and practical impact—not computational scale. Winning applications should be built entirely within Free Tier limits.

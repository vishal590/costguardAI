## How to use this doc

1. **Before writing the article:** Work through “Article & submission” and “Preparation & checklist”; use the quick list as a prompt list.
2. **Before publishing:** Confirm “Eligibility & rules” and “Timeline & logistics” (deadline, timezone).
3. **After publishing:** Focus on “Community voting” and “Competitive edge” for sharing and messaging.
4. **If you reach Top 50:** Use “Judging & criteria” and “What should I prepare if I make Top 50?” for the revised article.
